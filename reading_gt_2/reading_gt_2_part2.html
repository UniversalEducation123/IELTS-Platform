Brookside College Library

Read the information below about a college library and answer Questions 14–20.

A Application forms can be obtained from the Library Information Desk. Complete the form and present it, along with a current student or staff ID card, at the Information Desk or Loans Counter. Library membership cards are only valid for the academic year and should be renewed before the start of the first semester.

B Choose your books and take them to the Loans Desk. If you are in a hurry, you can use the Express Checkout. Just follow the instructions on the machine. The Express Checkout can be used for books and periodicals. Audio-visual loans must be checked out at the Loans Desk.

You cannot borrow items without showing your Library card.

C Items can be borrowed for 4 weeks. However, if another borrower places a hold on the item, you will have to return it within a week of receiving the notification of a hold from the Library. Failure to do this may result in a fine. Fines accumulate at a rate of £1.00 per week per item.

D Students and general staff may borrow up to 12 items. Academic staff can borrow additional items up to a total of 25, but only in semester breaks.

E Current loans can either be checked in the Library, or can be accessed through Brookside College's online Library catalogue. Students can log in using their student ID number.

Please note that you need to have a Personal Identification Number (PIN) to access the online catalogue. If you do not have a PIN, please see the Library's "Creating a Personal Identification Number" leaflet (available at the Library entrance).

F Items can be renewed twice, unless requested by another borrower. You can renew items either:

  1. by phoning the Loans Desk.
  2. via the online Library catalogue.
  3. in person at the Library.

G Items can be returned outside Library hours by using the return chutes located at the Library entrance.

H If you have any questions regarding the Library, then either talk to Library staff in person or check the Library website at www.brooksidecollege.co.uk/library.

Warner Language School (WLS) – Activity Afternoon

Every Friday afternoon at WLS is Activity Afternoon. Students need to sign up for one of the following options. Activities and lists are changed every five weeks.

Soccer

Want to represent WLS in inter-college soccer matches? Or do you just want a little bit of exercise? Then the soccer activity is the one for you. Students meet outside Cashmere Park (around the corner from the college) at 1.30 p.m. No soccer boots required, just sports shoes. Remember to bring a drink bottle, it gets warm running around!

IELTS Preparation

If you want to study at university in Australia, then this activity will give you plenty of practice and tips on how to approach the IELTS exam. Under the guidance of experienced IELTS teacher/examiner, Faith Cooper, you would undoubtedly see your results improve. You will need to buy a textbook. Details of this from Faith in week 1. Students must be at Intermediate level to take this course.

Everyday English

This course aims to help students communicate better in social and everyday situations. You will get lots of practice at speaking and listening to English that native speakers use. Learn how you express yourself naturally, but politely in English. Your activity also includes learning useful idioms and slang. Most of the lessons take place in the classroom, but some afternoons will include trips into the city to cafes, shops and other public places.

Culture Club

Want to learn more about the city of Warner and visit places that are often overlooked by visitors? Then sign up for this activity. Culture Club involves visiting interesting and cultural venues, including the Art Gallery, Channel 12 TV Station, St Edward's Cathedral and the Performing Arts Centre. Some costs are involved with this activity, so you need to be prepared to pay for this before signing up. Students meet at the City Rocket bus stop at 1.15 p.m.

Pronunciation

An important part of communicating well in English is pronunciation. This activity will help you to identify the sounds that are difficult for you, and show you how to correct them. You will also learn about English intonation and rhythm, which will help to improve your listening skills as well. A useful activity for all levels.

Computer Activity

This activity gives you the opportunity to use the Internet to learn English. Students can surf the Worldwide Web and use the numerous websites dedicated to English language learning. There are a maximum of 18 places on this activity, so you will need to sign up early to guarantee your choice. Please note that students are not allowed to send emails in any language other than English during the activity.

Swimming (only available Dec–Mar)

Enjoy relaxing and swimming in the Warner Olympic-sized outdoor pool. Students need to pay for entry and bus fare (total of $3 with student card). Learn-to-Swim program available at own cost (an additional $8 per lesson). Students meet at the City Rocket bus stop at 1.30 p.m.
