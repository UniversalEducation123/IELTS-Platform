Terms and Conditions of Enrolment

Full-fee paying international students are required to study full-time and comply with visa regulations. Students must maintain at least 90% attendance. The college is required to notify immigration authorities if attendance is unsatisfactory.

Students must have adequate English proficiency or undertake ELICOS before beginning their course. Students must also have adequate financial means and participate in orientation.

Note: You are advised to read and understand the conditions set out in the government acceptance advice form when you sign the declaration because you are required to comply with those conditions as an international student in Australia.

FEES

Application Fee

The application fee is $100 and is non-refundable.

Course Fees

The course fees set for 1997/1998 are:

Certificate Courses: A$ 10,200 per year

Advanced Certificate Courses: A$ 10,200 per year

Associated Diploma Courses: A$ 10,200 per year

ELICOS: A$ 5,950 for 20 weeks

VCE: A$ 5,600 for 20 weeks

The annual fee includes full cost of tuition and educational services provided by the college. Textbooks, equipment, tools, stationery and individual requirements that you may need in your studies are your responsibility.

Details of fee payment are provided in the provisional acceptance letter. Fees must be paid by the specified date in the letter.

All tuition fees must be paid by bankdraft and made payable to King George's College of TAFE.

Refund Policy

Application Fees

The application fee will not be refunded if the application is withdrawn after an offer of place has been made.

Application fees will be refunded if the application is rejected or if the course is cancelled by the college.

Course Fees

If a student withdraws after payment of fees, the following will apply

1. If notice of withdrawal is received by the college less than two (2) weeks before the course commences, no refund is given.

2. If written notice of withdrawal is given to the college less than four (4) weeks but more than two (2) weeks before the commencement of the course, 50 per cent of the course fee will be refunded.

3. If written notice of withdrawal is received by the college at least (4) weeks before the course commencement, 80 per cent of the tuition fee will be refunded.

All notices of withdrawal must be made in writing and addressed to the Manager, International Student Programs.

The notice of withdrawal should state name, course, date of commencement and reason for withdrawal.

TRANSFER TO ANOTHER INSTITUITION

Applications for transfer must be made in writing to International Student Programs stating raseons, and a copy of the accepatance letter from the receiving institution must be attached. Fees to be transferred will be subject to the refund policy.

Note: The college will not be responsible for any monies made payable to any agent.
