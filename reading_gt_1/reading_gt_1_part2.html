G

The college is a friendly place and has a pleasant, relaxed atmosphere. The few regulations that are enforced are mainly a matter of common sense, concerned with respecting the rights of fellow students and staff. For students studying practical, job training courses, it is compulsory to wear clothing which is appropriate to their workplace.

Terms and Conditions of Enrolment

Full-fee paying international students are required to study full-time and comply with visa regulations. Students must maintain at least 90% attendance. The college is required to notify immigration authorities if attendance is unsatisfactory.

Students must have adequate English proficiency or undertake ELICOS before beginning their course. Students must also have adequate financial means and participate in orientation.

Application Fee

The application fee is A$100 and is non-refundable.

Application fees will be refunded if an application is rejected or if the course is cancelled by the college.

Course Fees 1997/1998

Certificate Courses: A$10 200 per year

Advanced Certificate Courses: A$10 200 per year

Associated Diploma Courses: A$10 200 per year

ELICOS: A$5 950 for 20 weeks

VCE: A$5 600 for 20 weeks

The annual fee includes tuition and educational services. Textbooks, equipment, tools, stationery and individual requirements are the responsibility of the student.

Payment of Fees

Details of fee payment are provided in the provisional acceptance letter. Fees must be paid by the specified date.

All tuition fees must be paid by bank draft payable to King George's College of TAFE.

Refund Policy

The application fee will not be refunded if a student withdraws after an offer has been made.

Application fees will be refunded if the application is rejected or if the course is cancelled by the college.

If a student withdraws less than two weeks before the course begins, no refund will be given.

If a student withdraws less than four weeks but more than two weeks before the course begins, 50% of the tuition fee will be refunded.

If a student withdraws at least four weeks before the course begins, 80% of the tuition fee will be refunded.

All notices of withdrawal must be made in writing to the Manager, International Student Programs.
