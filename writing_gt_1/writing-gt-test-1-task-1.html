Writing Task 1

You should spend about 20 minutes on this task.

You recently received a letter from your bank that should have contained a new credit card. Unfortunately, the card was not in the letter.

Write a letter to your bank manager. In your letter,

  • explain what has happened
  • suggest the credit card should be cancelled for security reasons
  • ask when a new credit card can be sent to you

You should write at least 150 words.

You do NOT need to write any addresses. Begin your letter as follows:

Dear Sir/Madam,

Your Answer
